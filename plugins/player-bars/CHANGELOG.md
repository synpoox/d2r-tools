# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-01

First public release, for Diablo II: Resurrected 3.3 under D2RLoader 1.3.1.

- Health and mana bars above your character.
- Hides the orbs and statues, and keeps the frame behind the buttons, also on the controller HUD.
- Click to walk where the orbs and statues were.
- Bars fill smoothly, and a lighter section shows what a healing or mana potion is still restoring. Rejuvenation potions show at once.
- Colors match the orbs.
- Settings file for size, colors, position (above or below the character) and numbers.
